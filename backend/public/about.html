Sathaye College

Sathaye College

PARLE TILAK VIDYALAYA ASSOCIATION'S SATHAYE COLLEGE (AUTONOMOUS) Nested in the heart of Vile Parle, Mumbai cultural hub Sathaye College (Autonomous) has been a centre of learning and community spirit since 1959. Established by the Parle Tilak Vidyalaya Association (1921), the college has grown from its Arts, Science, and Commerce roots into a multidisciplinary institute offering programs in Management, IT, Mass Media, and Research. Alongside academics, it has nurtured talent in theatre, music, dance, debate, fine arts, sports, and social engagement, producing many of Mumbai's finest artists and leaders. Recognized as Best College of the Year (University of Mumbai, 2013–14) and lauded for its NCC, NSS, and Vocational Programs, the college lives by its motto "पूर्णता गौरवाय" (Excellence for Glory). Its vibrant intercollegiate festivals continue to inspire creativity, leadership, and cultural exchange.

The story of ASTRA

Sathaye College has long celebrated the creativity, talent and dynamism of its students through a vibrant tradition of intra-and intercollegiate festivals. This year, carrying forward that legacy while also reaching for new horizons, we present its unified grand intercollegiate fest Astra, uniting the spirit of creativity, competition and collaboration under one vibrant banner. By bringing together the diverse strengths of its associations from literary arts to performing arts, fine arts to sports, Astra is where talent meets opportunity and dreams take flight. It's more than a festival! It's a leap beyond horizons, a celebration of hope, unity and limitless possibilities.

We warmly invite you to be a part of our Inter-Collegiate Fest Astra 2025—a vibrant celebration of creativity, talent, and youthful spirit. This festival offers an excellent platform to showcase your abilities, express your ideas, and connect with students from diverse institutions. Join us in this exciting journey filled with competitions, performances and cultural exchange. Your participation will not only add energy and enthusiasm to the fest but also make it a true success. Come share your passion, discover new friendships, and create memories that will last a lifetime! We look forward to your enthusiastic participation and to celebrate this festival of talent together.

In perspective

Arts. Culture. Talent. Creativity.

ASTRA celebrates the timeless power of myth- where gods, heroes, and legends come alive through culture, art, literature, sports, gaming, and performance. Rooted in the Vedas, Puranas, Indian Mythology and so on, the festival reimagines ancient stories in a modern spirit, turning every event into a bridge between tradition and today's youth. From enchanted worlds like Hogwarts to the mysteries of mermaids, vampires, and werewolves, from Robin Hood's valor to Disney's fairy tales, from witches to Thor's hammer—ASTRA shows how myths shape every culture, making imagination the ultimate stage of inter-college creativity. Beyond India, ASTRA draws from Norse, Greek, Roman, Celtic, Egyptian, and other mythologies.
